## About me

Violinist based in Setagaya. I started at six, went through a conservatory preparatory course, and now play in two amateur orchestras while teaching on weekends.

## What I play

- **Orchestra**: concertmaster of Aurora Chamber Orchestra since 2019; first violin in the Shibuya Game Music Orchestra.
- **Chamber music**: string quartets above all. Dvořák's "American" quartet is my favourite.
- **As an extra player**: happy to join an orchestra for one concert when its rehearsal plan is clear.
