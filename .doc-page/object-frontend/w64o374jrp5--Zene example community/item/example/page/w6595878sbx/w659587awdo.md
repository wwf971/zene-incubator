## Who we are

Aurora is an amateur chamber orchestra of about thirty players, founded in 2015. We rehearse on Sunday afternoons in Setagaya and give two concerts a year.

## Joining

Apply here with what you play. We invite you to a rehearsal first; after it, the section and the conductor decide together. There is a monthly fee of 3,000 yen for the halls.

## This season

Dvořák's ninth symphony for the Winter Concert. We are looking for two violas.
